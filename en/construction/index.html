Frequently asked questions

How long does a project take?
Prototype — 1-3 days, a complete system — 1-2 weeks depending on complexity. ETL systems and custom ERPs — 3-6 weeks.
What technologies do you use?
Python, Google Apps Script, Excel VBA, Node.js (Fastify/Express), SQL/MariaDB/SQLite, aiogram 3, REST APIs of marketplaces (Wildberries, Ozon, Yandex Market, Amazon), Streamlit, Telegram Bot API.
How do you work?
Remotely worldwide. I work with individuals and businesses. Payment via bank transfer or cryptocurrency. Engagement details are agreed individually.
Who owns the code?
The client owns it fully. After payment I hand over source code, access, documentation, and video tutorials. No subscriptions or vendor lock-in.
Is there support after delivery?
2 weeks of free support and minor changes are included. After that — hourly rate or monthly retainer.
Can you integrate with ERPs like 1C, SAP, or QuickBooks?
Yes: via REST API, OData, Excel/CSV export, or direct database connection. ERP integration with marketplaces, CRMs, and Telegram is feasible.
How much does it cost?
Depends on scope: simple Google Sheets automation — from $200, custom CRM — from $1,000, ETL system with backend — from $1,800. Exact price — after the spec.
